## Capability ladder

For each topic, move only when there is evidence:

1. **Recognize** -- can define the terms when prompted.
2. **Explain** -- can derive the model without notes.
3. **Apply** -- used the model to predict, inspect, implement, or measure something.
4. **Judge** -- can compare alternatives for a stated workload and defend the trade-off.
5. **Teach** -- can explain the idea clearly and answer follow-up questions.

At the end of Weeks 4 and 8, choose three topics and record the current level plus a link or
description of the evidence. The goal is not for every topic to reach Level 5. The goal is to make
the difference between familiarity and usable judgment visible.
